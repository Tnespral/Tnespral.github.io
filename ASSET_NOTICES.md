# Asset notices

## Moorea bleaching sequence

`src/assets/coral-bleaching-stages.png` is Figure 1 from Boilard et al. (2020),
*Defining Coral Bleaching as a Microbial Dysbiosis within the Coral Holobiont*.
The website uses the two reef-slope panels photographed by Y. Chancerelle
(CRIOBE, Moorea) before and during the 2016 mass bleaching event.

- Article and figure: https://doi.org/10.3390/microorganisms8111682
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The article is published under CC BY 4.0. The project page credits the
photographer, institution, paper and licence next to the interactive sequence.
CSS crops the two panels from the original figure; it does not synthesize or
recolour the bleaching transition.

## NOAA coral-bleaching photograph

`public/images/noaa-coral-bleaching.jpg` is a 1280 × 960 derivative of Wendy
Cover/NOAA's 2015 photograph from the National Marine Sanctuary of American
Samoa. It is used beside a locally computed HSV saturation channel.

- File page and attribution: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NMSAS_-_Coral_Bleaching_(30668757294).jpg
- Original source: https://www.flickr.com/photos/onms/30668757294

The file page identifies the photograph as public domain because it was made
as part of a NOAA employee's official duties. The saturation panel is clearly
labelled as a derived input explanation rather than a model output.

## Model figures

Files under `public/figures/coral` are aggregate plots generated by the clean
model repository. They contain no raw coral photographs, file paths, manifests
or model checkpoints. Attention overlays are excluded from the automatic sync
until their underlying image rights are checked individually.

## Porous-media validation figures

Files under `public/figures/porous-media` are purpose-cropped renderings from
two recorded validation examples in Tiago Fernández-Nespral's individual
Machine Learning in Science project. The website uses them to compare the
input mask, target field, final prediction and a derived relative-error map.

The private dataset, raw arrays, competition files and trained checkpoint are
not distributed. These PNGs are presentation assets only and cannot be used to
  reconstruct a complete sample or dataset.

## T.E.S.T. biosensing figures

Files under `public/figures/biosensing` come from Tiago Fernández-Nespral's
2025 T.E.S.T. project archive. The website uses one representative microscopy
frame, a development screenshot of the offline analysis interface and a
photograph of the assembled prototype. It does not publish the recorded image
sequences, tracking outputs, calibration measurements or other assay data.

## Burglary forecast figures

Files under `public/figures/burglary` are rendered from a forward-only monthly
evaluation on an aggregate 500 m London grid. They show a probability surface,
binary observed cells and aggregate performance over time; they do not contain
the source tables or exact Police.uk point records.
