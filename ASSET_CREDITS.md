# Image credits

- `public/images/healthy-staghorn-noaa.jpg` is a resized derivative of
  [FKNMS staghorn coral (48770939486)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FKNMS_staghorn_coral_(48770939486).jpg),
  photographed by Greg McFall/NOAA. The source image is a United States
  government work in the public domain.
- `public/images/noaa-coral-bleaching.jpg` is a resized derivative of
  [NMSAS – Coral Bleaching (30668757294)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NMSAS_-_Coral_Bleaching_(30668757294).jpg),
  photographed by Wendy Cover/NOAA. The source image is a United States
  government work in the public domain.
- `src/assets/coral-bleaching-stages.png` is Figure 1 from
  [Boilard et al. (2020), *Defining Coral Bleaching as a Microbial Dysbiosis within the Coral Holobiont*](https://doi.org/10.3390/microorganisms8111682).
  The reef-slope photographs shown by the website were taken by Y. Chancerelle
  (CRIOBE, Moorea) before and during the 2016 mass bleaching event. The article
  and figure are licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The site presents
  the two source panels through CSS cropping without altering the source pixels.

The watermarked stock photograph supplied as a visual reference is not used in
the website.

- Files under `public/figures/porous-media` are cropped renderings of two
  validation examples produced during Tiago Fernández-Nespral's individual
  Machine Learning in Science project. They show binary masks, targets,
  predictions and derived error maps without distributing the source arrays or
  competition dataset.

- Files under `public/figures/biosensing` are Tiago Fernández-Nespral's project
  materials from the 2025 T.E.S.T. biosensing project: one microscopy frame, a
  development screenshot of the offline analysis interface and a photograph of
  the assembled measurement prototype. No raw recording sequence or assay data
  is distributed through the website.

- Files under `public/figures/burglary` are aggregate 500 m grid renderings and
  performance plots generated from Tiago Fernández-Nespral's rolling model
  evaluation. The underlying records come from Police.uk:
  “Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
  Licence v3.0.” Raw records, exact published coordinates and model checkpoints
  are not distributed through the website. Source: Office for National
  Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0. Contains OS
  data © Crown copyright and database right 2026.
